OpenTS credits

OpenTS is maintained by the OpenTS contributors, building on the work of Westwood Studios.

Client and presentation

The launcher and client come from CnCNet. The client was created by Rampastring for Dawn of the Tiberium Age (DTA) and is maintained by the CnCNet client contributors. Its interface uses Rampastring.XNAUI and MonoGame.

Our layouts, artwork, sounds, fonts, map previews and translations are based on the CnCNet Tiberian Sun client package, including the work of Rampastring, Bittah Commander and its other contributors. See the original package's full credits.

OpenTS-specific configuration and branding are maintained in the OpenTS client package.
